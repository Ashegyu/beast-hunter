# Alpha 0.65 monster sprite provenance

Nine new species were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool from the user's five monster encyclopedias, using the established Alpha 0.45 wolf sprite sheet only as the style/layout reference. No CLI/API fallback or procedural painting was used. The five original concept images are retained in `references/`; exact input hashes, subject roles, full initial prompts, two revision prompts, and final source hashes are in [prompts.json](sandbox:/workspace/sites/beast-hunter-motion/art/monsters065/prompts.json).

Each source contains five direction rows (south, southeast, east, northeast, north) and five pose columns (walk A, walk B, attack windup, attack contact, corpse). West-facing directions use the game's existing mirror rules. No additional independent hurt frame was generated; hurt uses the existing renderer's attack-windup pose plus recoil/flash reaction. These are deliberately compact two-frame movement/two-frame attack animations, not high-frame-count full animation cycles.

All nine original sheets were visually inspected. The bat was revised to remove canine anatomy inherited from the style reference, and the leopard was revised to improve outer tail/paw margins. Every final source has 25 separate opaque body cores and actual alpha transparency. No opaque body core touches a source edge. The generated spider/mushroom/wisp include fine edge pixels or attached effects; these original pixels are retained. Some source-edge pixels have very low residual alpha (maximum 22/255 on the devourer), so source validation distinguishes opaque body continuity from faint halo pixels rather than claiming a uniformly empty outer border.

`tools/pack-monsters065.py` performs technical extraction and packing only: identify the 25 largest body cores, use measured row bands for mushroom spores and detached crystal arms, assign each entire opaque detached component to its closest body connection, then recover the antialiased edge RGBA from the nearest assigned part, crop/pad, establish feet pivots and walk-derived anatomical scale, and shelf-pack three regional atlases. The row bands keep high effects from being attributed to the previous row's feet; whole-component ownership prevents a long detached ice arm from being split between an attack pose and the neighboring corpse. It never repaints, rescales, recolors, or regenerates source pixels. Selected source RGBA is byte-exact. Atlas WebP files are lossless and verified by byte-equal decoded RGBA. Only WebP delivery files are included in `dist/assets`; the manifest preserves logical PNG names through its existing delivery map. No established Alpha 0.45 atlas, direction mapping, or clip geometry is replaced.

The reproducible per-source SHA-256, native transparency fraction, per-direction body heights, all 225 crop/core rectangles and pivots, per-frame RGBA hashes, and atlas sizes/hashes are recorded in `tools/reports/alpha065-monsters.json`. These are asset geometry/encoding checks. They do not measure browser rendering time, GPU memory residency, or actual gameplay frame rate.
